SETUP LAB — demo seed data

Files:
- products.json: product records matching Product / Products in src/types.ts
- categories.json: categories matching Category / Categories
- kits.json: ready-made kits matching SetupKit / SetupKits

Important:
- Prices are illustrative RUB demo values, not live offers or verified market prices.
- Product specifications are starter/demo content and should be verified before any real commercial use.
- Image paths are local placeholders. Add corresponding files under public/images/products/ and public/images/kits/, or replace paths with hosted image URLs.
- ProductUseCase values use: work, study, gaming, content-creation.
- Kit productIds refer to product ids in products.json.
